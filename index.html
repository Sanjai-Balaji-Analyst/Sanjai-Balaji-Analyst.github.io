Predicting Cybersecurity Threat Severity

MSC DISSERTATION · R · CRISP-DM · HYPOTHESIS TESTING

Four algorithms, four targets, five-fold cross-validation — sixteen configurations, all negative R². Rather than assume a modelling mistake, I built a five-stage diagnostic protocol to interrogate the dataset itself: distribution testing, correlation screening, an internal-consistency check, and permutation testing.

Every configuration fell below the zero-skill baseline — schematic, not exact per-model values (see report for the full table).

The dataset was synthetic and internally inconsistent — a composite "control score" turned out not to be a function of the components it's defined by. The contribution is a validation protocol, runnable in under an hour, before anyone invests weeks modelling a dataset that can't support it.

Predicting Star Performance in Networks

R · LASSO · LOGISTIC REGRESSION / LDA

Tested elite-learning, structural-holes, and technological-breadth theories of high performance in collaboration networks.

Standardised coefficients — career length and elite exposure dominate; cohesion is slightly negative.

Tight, closed elite networks correlated with lower performance, not higher — favouring Burt's structural-holes theory over pure cohesion.

Follow-up study: does research impact come from idea novelty, or from network structure and collaboration scale?

Endoscopy Unit Capacity Planning

R (ompr / GLPK) · MILP

10 rooms, 26 weeks, minimising setup + operating cost against demand and clinician-availability constraints. 1,300 variables, 780 binary.

Relative diagnostic hours scheduled, by room size, in the optimal solution.

The cost-optimal schedule leans almost entirely on small rooms, leaving large rooms idle — cheap, but not necessarily the utilisation a hospital manager actually wants.
